The Story of KZ2

The Genesis & Legacy: Beginning in my sophomore year, I was helping a friend to create and run a business, which initially was managed by paper and text messages. I originally built a Java/Spring backend that used Selenium to bridge the gap between platforms like Yelp and the business. This legacy system effectively turned static lead-generation sites into a 24/7 automated sales force.

The Evolution (Moving ERP): As the business scaled, I migrated the infrastructure to a modern Next.js/TypeScript stack. This wasn't just a rewrite; it was an architectural shift. I replaced fragmented spreadsheets with a custom Full-Stack CRM that serves as the "brain" of the company.

The Intelligent Logistics Engine

The core algorithm solves a complex multi-variable problem: balancing multiple trucks, crews of 2-7 people, and geographic "lanes." It calculates real-time availability by analyzing:

  • Constraint-Based Scheduling: Matches truck size, equipment requirements, crew availability, and team sizes to specific job complexities.
  • Route Optimization: Minimizes "deadhead" (idle) driving miles and maximizes team utilization.
  • Dynamic Pricing: A long-distance calculator that fetches live market data for hotels, flights, and truck rentals to generate profitable, instant quotes.

Feature Ecosystem:

  • Automated Client Lifecycle: From an AI-driven quoting tool to Electronic Contracts and automated SMS/Email confirmation workflows via Twilio.
  • Financial Suite: Integrated payroll, expense tracking, and cash-flow analytics that replaced traditional accounting software.
  • Risk Mitigation: Automated "Readiness Checks" the day before a move and a cross-referenced blacklist database to protect the business.

Business Impact:

  • 80% reduction in administrative overhead.
  • 150% revenue growth achieved during the pandemic through hyper-efficient logistics.
  • 24/7 Lead Capture: Zero human intervention required from initial inquiry to finalized quote.

State Hearings Video Ingestion Engine

The Mission: US State legislative sessions produce thousands of hours of video that are difficult to search or index. I built a high-reliability pipeline to ingest, store, and transcribe this data for public transparency.

Zero-Disk Streaming Architecture: To handle large media files without scaling hardware costs, I designed the system to use yt-dlp to extract streams and pipe them directly to AWS S3. By bypassing local disk storage, the engine remains lightweight and prevents "disk full" crashes during long hearings.

System Reliability & Lanes

The engine follows a strict state machine to ensure no data is lost:

  • Lane Orchestration: Processes are isolated by state and branch (e.g., MI Senate vs. MI House), allowing for horizontal scaling and distinct logging.
  • AI Transcription: Integrated with Deepgram to process S3 media, with results automatically indexed in PostgreSQL.
  • Self-Healing: Includes an "abandoned job" reporting tool to identify videos that exceed retry limits for manual intervention.

Technical Impact:

  • Eliminated manual monitoring of legislative calendars via automated scrapers (Cheerio).
  • Scalable to all 50 states through process isolation.
  • Success-rate tracking and real-time health reporting.
